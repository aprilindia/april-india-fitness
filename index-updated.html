CLIENT TRANSFORMATIONS

Progress looks different for everyone.

These transformations reflect real coaching experiences shared with permission. Results vary by individual.

BODY COMPOSITION

Changing the strategy, not simply doing more.

A client was under-eating and doing high volumes of cardio while trying to reduce body fat.

We increased food intake, prioritised protein, shifted training towards resistance work and maintained balanced cardio.

Outcome: reduced body fat and increased muscle mass.
NUTRITION & BEHAVIOUR

Moving away from restrictive dieting.

Coaching focused on balanced daily nutrition, achievable weekly goals, progressive activity and consistent support.

Outcome: more than 25kg of collective weight loss across two similar client journeys.
CONFIDENCE & CONSISTENCY

Making the plan work for the person.

Coaching focused on mindset, consistency, confidence, sustainable eating and appropriate exercise within the scope of coaching.

Outcome: 10kg weight loss, improved gym confidence and healthier eating habits.

THE EXPERIENCED JOURNEY

My own journey taught me why sustainable change matters.

After years of FAD diets and learning first-hand how difficult it can be to take on a fitness journey alone, I moved from being a client to becoming a coach.

My own health transformation showed me how strongly physical wellbeing can influence confidence, energy and how we show up in everyday life and work.

Alongside coaching, I spent more than six years working in Product Management across technology environments. That experience strengthened my skills in data, project delivery, stakeholder communication, problem solving and turning ideas into practical solutions.

Today I bring both sides together: structured, analytical thinking with a genuinely human coaching style.
