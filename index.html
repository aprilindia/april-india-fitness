Professional experience.
Personal understanding.

I'm April, an independent Health & Wellness Coach. Alongside more than three years of 1:1 coaching, I bring over seven years of experience in technology and product management.

Working in fast-paced professional environments gave me first-hand insight into competing priorities, career pressures and the importance of confidence and sustainable performance. My own experience of making health and wellbeing a priority has shaped the practical, personal approach I now bring to coaching.

I believe progress should fit real life. My role is to help people understand what works for them, build consistency and become more confident in taking ownership of their wellbeing.
