CLIENT CASE STUDIES

Results that go beyond the scales.

Client examples are anonymised and reflect my coaching experience across different ages, goals and circumstances.

60+ • BODY COMPOSITION

Improving body composition without doing more.

The challenge: A petite client was under-eating and doing high volumes of cardio while trying to reduce body fat.

The approach: Increased food intake, prioritised protein, shifted training towards resistance work and maintained balanced cardio.

Outcome: Reduced body fat and increased muscle mass, while continuing with a sustainable plan.

“More effort isn't always the answer. Sometimes the strategy needs to change.”
30s • NUTRITION & BEHAVIOUR

From restrictive dieting to sustainable eating.

The challenge: A history of restrictive/fad diets, under-eating during the day and binge eating at night.

The approach: Balanced daily nutrition, achievable weekly goals, progressive activity and consistent coaching support.

Outcome: Across two clients with similar restrictive-dieting patterns, more than 25kg of collective weight was lost.

“The behaviour behind the goal matters as much as the goal itself.”
30s • COMPLEX CIRCUMSTANCES

Personalised coaching through a complex journey.

The challenge: A client with significant health and lifestyle considerations alongside low confidence and an inconsistent relationship with food.

The approach: Focused on mindset, consistency, confidence, sustainable eating and appropriate exercise within my coaching scope.

Outcome: 10kg weight loss, improved gym confidence and healthier eating habits.

“The person behind the numbers matters.”
